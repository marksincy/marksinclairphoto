Lines in the Sky

\
\ Sydney CBD & Barangaroo\ •\ Fuji X-T5 & 23mm\ •\ 4 min read\
\
\ \
\
\ \
\

Crown Sydney rising through Barangaroo morning light.

\
\ \
\

\ Walking through Barangaroo on an overcast morning completely changes how light bounces off glass and steel. There is a geometric rhythm to the high-rise corridors that only reveals itself when you deliberately stop looking at street level and point the lens skyward.\

\ \

\ The sheer verticality of Crown and the neighbouring towers creates sharp, mathematical silhouettes against a soft grey sky. When shooting architecture on street walks, perspective distortion is both the biggest challenge and the most powerful compositional tool.\

\ \
\ "The eye seeks geometry in chaos. In a city of millions, looking up offers a quiet, abstract stillness that the crowded pavements never provide."\
\ \

Reflections & Perspective

\

\ By moving tightly against the building perimeters and shooting upwards with the 23mm, parallel architectural lines converge into dramatic focal points. The overcast cloud layer acts as a massive softbox, removing harsh specular glare and giving the reflective glass panels deep tonal gradients.\
