  • About

Light, geometry & coastal stillness.

I am a documentary, street, and landscape photographer based on the Central Coast of New South Wales, Australia. My work focuses on finding quiet order within chaotic environments—whether tracking converging light through high-rise corridors or observing tidal textures along the coastline.

"Observation is about patience. Giving everyday scenes the breathing room to reveal their underlying geometry."

Alongside visual series, I write regular photo essays documenting local walks, film workflows, and regional explorations across the Coast and abroad.

Gear & Cameras

  • Digital: Fujifilm X-T5
  • Everyday / Street: Fujifilm X100VI
  • Film: Olympus OM-1 (35mm)
  • Preferred Stock: Ilford HP5 Plus, Kodak Tri-X

Visual Influences

  • Daido Moriyama: High-contrast grain & street rhythm
  • Alex Webb: Layered light & complex colour
  • Saul Leiter: Reflections, abstraction & quiet moments
